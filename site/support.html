Support

We're here to help. Email info@teamclubhq.com and we'll reply within one business day.

I got an invite link from my coach

Install ClubHQ, create an account, then tap the link again, or choose I'm a parent or guardian and paste the code from the invite page.

How do I add a second child?

Open the invite link again from the same account and add the next child. Each child can be on a different team.

How does my teenager get their own login?

Players 13 and older: a parent or the head coach opens the player's profile, taps Create player login code, and sends the code to the player.

I didn't get the confirmation email

Check spam and promotions folders for mail from teamclubhq.com. Then try signing in again to resend.

Cancel Coach Pro

iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → ClubHQ.

Delete my account

In the app: More → Delete Account.
