What I'll build

  • Facebook: profile picture, cover photo with mobile and desktop safe zones, post and story templates, event cover
  • Instagram: profile picture, 4:5 feed post templates, story and Reel cover templates, highlight covers
  • TikTok: profile picture, video cover template, and on-screen safe-zone guides
  • Six launch posts, each written in your voice for all three platforms
  • App Store screenshot frames, built from your existing app screens
  • HANDOFF-iOS.md and HANDOFF-Site.md, written for Claude Code in Xcode

Check before approving

  • Do the light and dark app mockups match the feel you want in the app?
  • Is the link preview's wording right?
  • Do you have your App Store app ID? It goes in the head snippet's apple-itunes-app tag. If not, I'll leave a placeholder.
