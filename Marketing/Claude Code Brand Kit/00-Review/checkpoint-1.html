Foundation: mark, icon, wordmark, color & type

Your “#3 Accent Arrow” concept, rebuilt as clean vector geometry. Everything here is generated from one source file, so any change you request ripples through every logo, lockup and icon at once. Approve or mark up the items at the bottom and I’ll move to Phase 2.

What changed from the concept, and why

The idea is intact: H + Q, navy / cyan / orange, the rising arrow. These are the refinements.

01

The word is out of the icon

iOS already prints “ClubHQ” under the icon. At 60px the word is unreadable mush and it shrinks the mark. The icon is now just the HQ mark, about 40% larger. See the side-by-side test below.

02

True vector, custom letters

The concept was a raster image with an unidentified font. The mark and the “ClubHQ” wordmark are now hand-built geometric outlines, so there's no font licence and nothing to convert. Every SVG opens in Illustrator.

03

Accessible color pairs

Bright cyan #00C2FF on white is 2.1:1, below the 4.5:1 that text needs. It stays the hero on dark, and a Deep Cyan #0077C2 carries links and buttons in light mode.

04

Built for iOS 27 icon modes

H, Q and arrow are separate shapes with a knockout gap, so in Phase 2 they become Icon Composer layers. Default, dark, tinted and clear modes then render properly, not as flattened screenshots.

The mark

The gaps between H, Q and arrow are real cut-outs, not painted navy, so the mark sits cleanly on any background.

Type

Outfit for headlines and marketing (free, OFL licence; its geometry matches the wordmark). Inter for website body text. In the app, SF Pro, Apple's system font, keeps Dynamic Type and accessibility for free. Outfit is used only for big moments like onboarding titles.
