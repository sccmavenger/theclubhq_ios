# Approved direction and acceptance criteria

Selected: **HQ Motion — Refined Balance (#3)**.

Keep:
- Strong, legible HQ monogram centered on navy.
- Orange athletic motion sweep, not a busy playbook diagram.
- Small X and O details only if readable at small icon sizes.
- `ClubHQ` exact casing for wordmark; `ClubHQ Team` remains the App Store name.
- Friendly, trustworthy, all-sports brand; not overly aggressive.

## Before release
1. Compare all curves against the **selected #3 raster reference**, and hand-adjust the vector; the SVG here is only a starting reconstruction.
2. Outline custom lettering or use an appropriately licensed font; document license.
3. Verify contrast, 60pt legibility, monochrome/tinted silhouette, dark and clear variants.
4. Check trademark distinctiveness and brand collision risk.
5. Request owner approval of final vector, exports and on-device previews before replacing existing assets.
